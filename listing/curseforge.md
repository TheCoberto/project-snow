## Description

Get your world ready for the holidays. Snowfall makes it snow across your plains, forests, taigas, hills and swamps, so snow piles up, rivers freeze and every leaf and blade of grass turns frosty white. When the season's over, switch to Snowfall Thaw and watch the snow melt away around you, without touching the ice and snow in your builds. Works on existing worlds.

**Snowfall**

Makes it snow in 33 temperate and cool biomes:
- **Plains and meadows**: plains (heaviest snow), sunflower plains, meadow, cherry grove
- **Forests**: forest, flower forest, dappled forest, all birch forests, both dark forests, pale garden
- **Taigas**: taiga, mega taigas and redwood taigas
- **Windswept hills**: all five extreme hills biomes
- **Swamps**: swamp and mangrove swamp
- **Water edges**: rivers and beaches

Grass, leaves and birch trees turn near-white so everything looks frosted. Hot, dry and ocean biomes (desert, badlands, savanna, jungle, oceans) stay as they are.

**Snowfall Thaw**

When winter's over, turn Snowfall off and Thaw on. Snow and ice open to the sky melt gradually around players; an area is mostly clear after about 5 minutes nearby.
- Only melts **ice** and **snow layers**. Packed ice, blue ice and snow blocks are safe.
- Only melts blocks **open to the sky**, so ice under a roof or inside a build is safe.
- Only melts in the biomes Snowfall changed, so frozen oceans and snowy tundras keep their ice.

Heads up: ice you placed outside on purpose in one of these biomes will melt too. Put a roof over it or mine it with Silk Touch first.

### Installation

**Starting winter**
1. Download the .mcaddon and open it. Minecraft imports all three packs.
2. Create or edit a world and turn on the Snowfall behavior pack (the resource pack is added with it).

**Ending winter**
1. Turn Snowfall off in the world's behavior and resource packs.
2. Turn on Snowfall Thaw and walk around your base until the snow is gone.
3. Turn Thaw off once spring has arrived.
